# 070.020 — `cosmos-llm-research`

The research vertical, built on `cosmos-llm-agent`. Everything that makes a
research tool a research tool, minus the interface.

## Scope

**In:** the six research agents, the research tool set, methodology preamble
assembly, citation extraction and attribution, quality metrics, output composition
into a `ResearchOutput`.

**Out:** the session store, the TUI, the CLI, config file loading. Those are the
harness's.

## Microsteps

### 070.020.010 — The six agents

`literature-review`, `data-analysis`, `hypothesis-testing`, `synthesis`,
`citation-specialist`, `methodology-review` — prompts and temperatures verbatim from
`old/AGENTS.md`, as `AgentEntry` registrations.

Prompts in `assets/` via `include_str!`, editable as prose.

### 070.020.020 — The research tool set

`web_search`, `fetch_url`, `extract_structured`, `cite`, plus phase 080's academic
search when it lands. `ToolEntry` registrations, so a consumer gets them by linking
the crate.

The generic halves live lower: `ssrf-guard`, `content-extract`, `json-schema-coerce`
([`../PLAN/005`](../PLAN/005.000.000-decomposition-principle.md)). This crate wires
them into tools.

### 070.020.030 — Citation integration

Depends on `citation` and `citation-style` (layer 1). Adds the LLM-specific part:
extracting citations from model output, marking them unverified, attributing them
to the tool results that produced them.

The attribution matters. A citation traceable to a search result is verifiable; one
that appeared in prose is not, and the distinction should survive into the output.

### 070.020.040 — `ResearchOutput`

The structure the harness's formatters consume: answer, shape (prose or tabular),
citations, metadata, usage, quality metrics, methodology record.

Defined here rather than in the harness, so a server or a different frontend
consumes the same type.

### 070.020.050 — Output composition, not writing

Compose the `Document` structure from
[`../PLAN/040.020.010`](../PLAN/040.020.000-prose-formats.md) — sections, ordering,
citation placement. Rendering to bytes stays in `tabular-emit` and the harness's
format writers.

Composition is domain logic (what belongs in a research report). Rendering is
mechanical. Different crates.

### 070.020.060 — Quality metrics

From [`../PLAN/090`](../PLAN/090.000.000-phase-quality.md), including the honesty
constraints: measured versus assessed, no composite grade by default, disclosure of
model self-assessment.

Those constraints are part of the design, not the presentation. Encoding them here
means every consumer inherits them rather than each frontend deciding whether to
disclose.

## Verification

- End-to-end research run against the mock provider, no harness involved.
- All six agents load and validate.
- Citations carry attribution to their source tool results.
- `ResearchOutput` round-trips through serde.
- Quality metrics carry their measured/assessed label at the type level.
